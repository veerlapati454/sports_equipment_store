Questions Athletes Ask First

Straight answers about testing, delivery, returns, and sizing

What does "Apex Verified" actually mean?

It means a product has cleared all four of our checkpoints: material sourcing audit, in-lab stress testing, athlete field trials, and final certification. If it doesn't pass every stage, it never appears in our catalog.

How does the 30-day performance trial work?

Train in your gear for up to 30 days. If it doesn't perform the way you expected, contact our support team and we'll arrange a return or exchange. Unlike most retailers, you don't have to keep it unworn to qualify.

How fast will my order arrive?

Every order is dispatched within 24 hours and tracked in real time, from our warehouse to your door. Delivery time after dispatch depends on your location and the shipping option you choose at checkout.

Can I get sizing advice before I buy?

Yes. Our support desk is staffed by certified coaches and trainers who can help you pick the right size and model for your sport, surface, and training load. Reach them any time through our contact page.

How are the sustainability figures calculated?

The percentages above show the share of our materials, packaging, and shipping routes that currently meet each standard. We update them as suppliers and routes change, so the numbers reflect where we are today, not where we hope to be.
