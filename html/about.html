Our Story

From a spare-room workshop to a global performance brand

Apex Athletics was founded by a small group of sports biomechanists and former professional athletes who were tired of gear that looked good in a store but broke down under real training loads.

The first product — a hand-built compression sleeve — was tested on a local marathon club before it ever had a name. That habit never left us: every material, sole, and stitch still goes through the same cycle of lab testing and athlete feedback before it reaches a shelf.

What We Stand For

The four commitments that shape every product decision we make

01

Evidence First

No product ships on a hunch. Every claim we make — energy return, moisture control, grip — is measured in a lab before it's printed on a box.

In practice: our Carbon-Kinetix sole's 87% energy-return figure is independently re-verified every production run.
02

Athlete-Led Design

We co-design with the coaches, physios, and competitors who use the gear daily — not just marketing teams guessing what looks fast.

In practice: 320+ clubs and academies feed us seasonal wear-and-tear reports we build into the next iteration.
03

Responsible Materials

Performance and sustainability aren't a trade-off to us — recycled fibers and low-impact polymers now run through most of our line.

In practice: 85% of our current lineup uses recycled textile fiber and zero-plastic packaging.
04

Stand Behind It

If gear doesn't hold up to real training, that's on us to fix — not the athlete's problem to work around.

In practice: every order carries our 30-day performance guarantee, no fine print.
