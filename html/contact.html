Before You Write In

The five things the desk gets asked most

How long does dispatch take?

Orders placed before 4:00 PM IST leave the Salem hub the same working day. Domestic delivery runs 2–4 days, international 6–9 days with GPS tracking on every parcel.

Can I return gear after training in it?

Yes. The 30-day performance guarantee covers used gear. Train in it, and if it doesn't improve your session, send it back — we cover return shipping within India.

Do you size footwear differently for wide feet?

The Carbon Flyer runs true to length but narrow through the midfoot. Mail the gear desk your usual size and foot width and we'll tell you whether to go half a size up.

What do club and academy orders need?

Send a squad list with sizes and your season start date. Orders above 15 units get tiered pricing, free name printing and a single consolidated dispatch.

Can I test gear at the Salem hub?

The fitting bay has a treadmill, a lifting platform and a short grip lane. Book a slot through the form above and a coach will keep an hour free for you.

Kitting out a squad?

Over 320 clubs and academies order through the team desk. Send a squad list and you'll get sizing sheets, tiered pricing and one dispatch date back within two working days.
