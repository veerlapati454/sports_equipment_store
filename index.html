About Apex Athletics

Forging the future of human athletic performance

Founded in 2014 by a team of sports biomechanists and former professional athletes, Apex Athletics was born out of a simple frustration: mainstream equipment wasn't keeping up with modern training demands.

We set out to create high-performance gear that seamlessly blends cutting-edge material science with ergonomic comfort. Today, Apex Athletics supplies sports equipment, footwear, and smart training accessories to over 500,000 athletes across 45 countries—from amateur marathoners to elite Olympic contenders.

Inside the engineering that powers modern world records

Footwear Innovation

Carbon-Kinetix™ Sole Architecture

Our flagship footwear features an ultra-lightweight full-length carbon fiber plate embedded within high-density nitrogen-infused foam. This dual-layer structure stores kinetic energy during footstrike and returns up to 87% of energy during toe-off, enabling longer strides with reduced calf strain.

  • 87% Verified Energy Return
  • 140g Ultra-Lightweight Build
  • Multi-surface HydroGrip Rubber
Apparel Technology

ThermoVent™ Moisture Control

Designed for extreme thermal regulation, ThermoVent micro-weaves expand when body temperature rises, increasing airflow by up to 300%. The hydrophobic interior layer wicks sweat away from the skin instantly, preventing chaffing during high-intensity training sessions.

  • 4-Way Seamless Elastic Weave
  • Anti-Odor Silver-Ion Infusion
  • UV 50+ Sun Protection Shield
Equipment Engineering

ProGrip-X™ Surface Composite

Utilized in our premium basketballs, weightlifting grips, and tennis handles, ProGrip-X utilizes a textured polyurethane matrix that absorbs moisture dynamically, maintaining maximum tactile control even during prolonged sweaty competition.

  • Precision Ergonomic Weighting
